Product Cards

Title and price now sit side-by-side, saving vertical space and letting shoppers scan faster.

A strikethrough compare-at price makes sale value immediately obvious, which drives urgency and conversion.

Colour swatches moved below the title keep the eye on the product image first, then guide shoppers into selecting a variant.

The size dropdown is now a standalone row, making it more prominent and less likely to be missed.

The quantity selector and "Add to Bag" button share a single horizontal row instead of stacking vertically, shortening the card and keeping the CTA visible without scrolling.

Overall, the redesign reduces cognitive load, surfaces key purchase drivers (sale price, variants) earlier, and creates a more compact, conversion-optimized layout.
